Our mission

Build useful software that makes the work around AI easier to understand, inspect and complete.

We develop products from problems encountered in practical AI engineering. Our first focus is document preparation for retrieval augmented generation.

Our product focus

Precidoc is RAinc's first product. It addresses the decisions around extracted text, document chunking, source references and export.

We are building the product iteratively. Development progress is recorded in our updates and public website repository.
