Precidoc brings extracted text, source comparison, chunk review and structured exports into a document preparation workspace.

Its current public interface supports digital PDF, DOCX, TXT and Markdown, with JSON or Markdown output. Feature availability can change during development.

Why start with documents?

Preparing documents for a retrieval pipeline takes more than choosing a chunk size. You need to inspect the text, keep its source references and check the result.

That is the problem behind Precidoc.
