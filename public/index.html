About RAinc

We build and own
our products.

RAinc is an independent product studio founded by Rizal Aditya. We develop software from problems encountered while working on AI systems.

Precidoc is our first product. It focuses on the preparation work that comes before documents enter a RAG pipeline.

Why document preparation?

Choosing a chunking strategy is only part of the task. You also need to check the extracted text, inspect the chunks, and know where each one came from.

Precidoc puts those steps in one workspace, with an export you can take into your own pipeline.
